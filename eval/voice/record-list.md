# Sentences to record for the voice term test

Ten questions a student might ask about fixtures/heart.png. Together they use all 19 part names Spark listed for the heart in the Oct 29 bake-off, and "aorta" three times, since the synthetic clip lost it ("Where is the order?").

Read each one once, at a normal pace, as a separate WAV: mono, 16 kHz or 24 kHz, the formats the file endpoint takes. Name them q01.wav to q10.wav.

1. Where is the aorta?
2. Does the left ventricle pump blood into the aorta?
3. Do the superior vena cava and the inferior vena cava both reach the right atrium?
4. Where are the tricuspid valve and the chordae tendineae?
5. Is the mitral valve between the left atrium and the left ventricle?
6. Where do the right pulmonary arteries and the left pulmonary arteries go?
7. Do the right pulmonary veins and the left pulmonary veins enter the left atrium?
8. Does the septum separate the right ventricle from the left ventricle?
9. Which leaves the aorta first, the brachiocephalic artery or the left common carotid artery?
10. Where are the semilunar valves and the left subclavian artery?
