Two published sheets and the phone

The heart and the Commons brain are real published sheets, version 4 of each, checked by the teacher. On their pages, point at the sheet or tap it to hear each part, from the same regions file the phone uses.

The heart

A labeled cross-section of the human heart: 11 touchable parts, 4 lines (three valves and the chordae tendineae), and one merge for the aortic arch.

The Commons brain
