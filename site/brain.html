The Commons brain

The lobes of the human brain, lateral view, drawn as one unbroken shape. The same pipeline as the heart: Muse Spark planned the parts, SAM 3.1 outlined them, and the teacher checked all 5 parts on the sheet and described two.

  • Move the pointer onto a part: a tone, then its name when you rest.
  • Click or tap a part to hear its name at once. Rest inside the outline, on no part, to hear the subject.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a part on the sheet or in the list, and press Enter. Escape stops speech.
  • The browser speaks only after a first click, tap or key press on the page.

This is a stand-in on a screen. The real product follows a fingertip on the printed, raised sheet with a phone camera, and plays the same tones and names. Questions need the live app and are not on this site.

  • The print PDF (US Letter, 39 KB). Print at 100 percent (“Actual size”) and check that the bar measures 100 mm.

The parts

From this sheet’s regions.json. Each button says the part’s name.

Touchable parts (5)

  • texture: dots. Teacher’s note: “The frontal lobe helps you plan, make choices and move your body.”
  • texture: lines.
  • texture: grid.
  • texture: solid. Teacher’s note: “The occipital lobe makes sense of what your eyes see.”
  • texture: cross.

Not on the sheet (1)

  • Olfactory bulb: left for the teacher. The pipeline’s note: every point was dropped by the guard.

Outline

Resting inside the brain’s outline, on no part, says “Brain”. This sheet was made for grade band 6 to 8.
